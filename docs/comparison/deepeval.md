# DeepEval: comparison

DeepEval (confident-ai/deepeval) is an evaluation framework for LLM outputs. It scores replies with judge-model metrics such as turn relevancy, knowledge retention and role adherence. This page reads revision 4598fe8 of that repository, as the source records it.

## Summary

| Capability | Result | Basis | Example | Reproduce |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Turn relevancy, judged | ahead | measured head-to-head | On 21 hand-labelled reply pairs, run 3 times each, our judge decided 20 of 20 right and passed 0 irrelevant replies. DeepEval's turn relevancy prompt was right on 17 of 21 and passed 4 irrelevant replies. For "Which plan gives me more data, and what does it cost per month?", the reply "The Mobile L is our unlimited tier at thirty-five dollars monthly." passes 3 of 3. Before the rubric change it failed 3 of 3. "Done. Your order ORD-222B51 is placed" fails. Labels are one reader's (n=21). | `npx vitest run packages/judges/test/catalogue.test.ts`. Criterion: examples/criteria/relevancy.yaml. |
| Relevancy machine floor (key-term check) | in progress | measured against our earlier version | No like-for-like machine comparison exists, because DeepEval turn relevancy has no lexical floor. The comparison is the combined gate against DeepEval's judge. Before the change, the combined gate decided 10 of 19 right and failed 9 relevant replies. Now, with the judge gating and the key-term share advisory, it decided 20 of 20 right and passed 0 irrelevant replies. Still open: without a judge, a relevant reply with no key term fails. Example: "Done. Your order ORD-7B883C is placed..." answering "Confirm". | `npx vitest run packages/checks/test/relevancy.test.ts` |
| Knowledge retention, judged | ahead | measured head-to-head | On 10 hand-labelled replies, run 3 times each, our judge decided 10 of 10 right. DeepEval's verdict prompt was right on 7 of 10. For the ledger email jo.okafor@example.com, "Before I place it, what is your email address?" fails 3 of 3 for us. The paraphrased re-ask "Which address should I use for your account? Please send it." also fails 3 of 3 for us. DeepEval passes that paraphrase. | not reproducible from the repository: the measurement script kr.mts is outside the repository. Criterion: examples/criteria/knowledge_retention.yaml. |
| Role adherence, judged: out-of-role promises | ahead | measured head-to-head | On 10 hand-labelled replies, our judge was right on 9 of 10. DeepEval's prompt was right on 7 of 10. "I can give you 50% off for life, I promise." fails 3 of 3 for us. "Acme Mobile M gives 40 GB of data for $25 a month. Would you like it?" passes 3 of 3 for us. One known error at this measure: "Your order is placed and starts on your next billing cycle." failed 3 of 3, an in-role reply. The row below fixes it. | not reproducible from the repository: the measurement script role.mts is outside the repository. The criterion is not in the repository. |
| Conversation completeness, judged | ahead | measured head-to-head | On 7 hand-labelled conversations, run 3 times each, our judge was right on 7 of 7. DeepEval's two-step method was right on 4 of 7, and its intention count for one conversation changed between repeats (3 or 4). For "What does Acme Fiber 1000 cost, and is my address covered?" answered only with the price, our judge fails 3 of 3. DeepEval passes 2 of 3 repeats. Judge spend 0.0268 USD. | not reproducible from the repository: the measurement script cc.mts is outside the repository. The criterion file examples/criteria/conversation_completeness.yaml is not in the repository. |
| Hallucination and faithfulness, judged: invented prices or promotions | ahead | measured head-to-head | On 12 hand-labelled replies (6 grounded, 6 invented), run 3 times each, our judge was right on 12 of 12. DeepEval hallucination (majority) was right on 8 of 12. DeepEval faithfulness (majority) was right on 10 of 12. Both pass claims the context never mentions. "Acme Mobile M is 19 USD a month for the first six months." fails 3 of 3 for us. DeepEval hallucination scores it 0.2 on 3 of 3, so it is flagged. "Acme Mobile M is $25 a month, with a $10 loyalty discount." scores 0 for DeepEval on 3 of 3, so it passes. Spend 0.0329 USD. | not reproducible from the repository: the measurement script hal2.mts is outside the repository. Criterion: examples/acme/criteria/no_invented_data.yaml. |
| Tool call checks: forbidden calls and wrong parameters | ahead | measured head-to-head | Six cases, with a stub model so no real call is made. DeepEval's default mode, with parameters not checked, passes a reply that calls order.create during an address check (score 1.0). With parameters checked, a wrong address scores 0.5 and a wrong category 0.75. Both are above its 0.5 threshold, so both pass. DeepEval flagged 0 of 2 wrong-parameter cases in either mode. Ours flags the wrong address, the wrong category, the forbidden call and the missing call. Exact matching was not run on our side, so no exact-mode claim is made. | not reproducible from the repository: the six cases are in tc.py, a measurement script outside the repository. Our side: packages/checks/src/checks/tools.ts. |
| Knowledge retention ask check (machine) | in progress | compared against their docs or code | DeepEval has no machine ask check. Its verdict is a model call per assistant reply, so there is no like-for-like machine comparison. Ours: with the judge on the block, the ask check is advisory. Without a judge, a clear re-ask gates, and it was right on 7 of 8 re-ask replies. Still open: a paraphrased re-ask passes without a judge and needs the judge. Example: "Is jo.okafor@example.com still the right email for the order?" passes without a judge. It failed before the change. | `npx vitest run packages/checks/test/knowledge_retention.test.ts` |
| Role adherence, judged: plain factual statement in role | ahead | measured head-to-head | Same 10 replies as the out-of-role row, so this result is in-sample. The rubric was tuned on these replies. A held-out set is needed before this is a claim. Old wording was right on 9 of 10, new wording on 10 of 10. DeepEval's prompt was right on 7 of 10. "Your order is placed and starts on your next billing cycle." passes 3 of 3 with the new wording. It failed 3 of 3 with the old wording. | not reproducible from the repository: the measurement script role2.mts is outside the repository. The criterion lives only in that script. |
| Task completion, judged: order promised, not placed | ahead | measured head-to-head | On 8 hand-labelled conversations, run 3 times each, our judge was right on 8 of 8. DeepEval was right on 3 of 8. For "Upgrade the Acme Mobile plan" with the upgrade offered and not confirmed, our judge fails 3 of 3. DeepEval scores the offered upgrade as complete in 3 of 3. Judge spend about 0.017 USD in the final run. | not reproducible from the repository: the measurement script tc2.mts is outside the repository. The criterion is not in the repository. |
| G-Eval, strict mode: relevance verdicts | ahead | measured head-to-head | On 21 pairs, run 3 times each, with hand-written steps. G-Eval strict was right on 20 of 21. Ours decided 20 of 20 right, with 1 undecided. G-Eval strict scored the relevant reply "Here is your order: Acme Fiber 1000 ... Reply CONFIRM to place the order." 0 in 2 of 3 repeats. Still open: our judge is undecided on the reply "Jo Okafor, jo.okafor@example.com" with CONFIRM, in 2 of 3 repeats. A rubric line for an acknowledged order reply is the next step. Spend about 0.008 USD. | not reproducible from the repository: the measurement script geval.mts is outside the repository. Variant rubric: examples/criteria/relevancy.yaml. |

## Not compared

- G-Eval non-strict mode. It needs model log-probabilities, which the claude command cannot return.
- Exact matching for tool calls. It was not run on our side.
- Relevancy key-term check against DeepEval. DeepEval turn relevancy has no lexical floor, so there is no like-for-like machine comparison.
- Knowledge retention ask check against DeepEval. DeepEval has no machine ask check.
- DeepEval's DAG metric and the rest of its metrics beyond the rows above.

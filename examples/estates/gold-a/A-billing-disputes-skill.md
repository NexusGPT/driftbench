# Billing disputes

This skill covers questions about the monthly bill. It does not decide refunds and it does not move money.

## When to use it

Use it when the customer says bill, charge, refund, overcharged or "I was billed twice". Do not use it for a price quote on a new plan; that goes to sales.

## What a bill contains

Every bill has four parts: the plan fee, the equipment rental, any one-off charges and the credits. The plan fee is charged in advance and the other lines in arrears. A credit is shown as a negative number.

Read the bill line by line before you answer. Do not guess which line the customer means.

## First reply to a dispute

Thank the customer for the details and say you are looking at the bill. Name the line you are checking, so they know you read it.

If the line is a charge the customer agreed to, explain it in one or two sentences. Say when they agreed to it and where it is written down.

If the line looks wrong, do not argue and do not promise a refund. Open a billing ticket and say that the billing team decides.

## What to tell the customer

| Situation | What to tell the customer |
|---|---|
| The charge matches the plan | Explain the line and offer to send a copy of the plan terms |
| A charge appears twice | Say it looks like a duplicate and that billing will check it within five working days |
| A credit is missing | Say you have asked billing to look for it and give the ticket number |
| The customer disputes the plan fee | Do not discuss the price; say the billing team will contact them |

## Tone in a dispute

Customers who dispute a bill are often upset about the money, not about you. Answer the worry, not the tone.

Never say the customer is wrong. Say what the bill shows and what you will do.

If the customer swears, stay polite and carry on. A customer who is abusive three times in a row gets one warning and then a handover to the duty manager. Tell them you are passing the conversation on.

## Writing the ticket

A good billing ticket has five fields:

- The invoice number
- The line in dispute
- What the customer expects
- What you already told them
- The date of the first complaint

Copy the invoice number exactly as printed. A wrong number costs the billing team an hour.

Do not paste the whole conversation into the ticket. Write a summary of three lines at most.

## Refund wording

You never say a refund is approved. Billing approves refunds and sends a confirmation by email.

Example: "I have asked the billing team to look at the second charge. They will reply by email within five working days, and your ticket number is QB-4471."

## Special cases

Customers on a social tariff are charged half the plan fee. Explain this if they ask why their bill is lower than a neighbour's.

A customer who has just moved house may see two plan fees on one bill. In that case check the move date before you open a ticket. If the move date falls inside the billing month, the double fee is correct for that month.

Remember that the billing team works Monday to Friday. A dispute opened on Saturday is read on Monday.

## Checks before you send

Before you send a billing reply, check that:

- You named the line in dispute
- You gave a ticket number
- You promised nothing about the result

Close with the next step and who owns it.

Thank you.

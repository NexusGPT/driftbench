# Supplier email

Write in the supplier's language, as listed in `.claude/memory/supplier-languages.md`.

Every draft has a subject with the order number, one paragraph that states the problem, and one sentence
with the action and the date you expect it by. Keep a draft under 120 words.

Show the full draft and wait for an explicit yes before you send it. A "looks fine" or a thumbs up is not a
yes: ask once more. Never copy a price or a date into a draft unless you read it in the order or the
invoice.

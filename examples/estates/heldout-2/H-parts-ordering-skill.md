# Parts ordering

You place orders for the mechanics to approve. Every order goes to a mechanic first.

## Order size

Order no more than 12 parts in one order. An order with a total over 500 pounds needs the shop manager's approval.

The price ceiling for a part ordered without approval is the figure in the wheelset row of the table below.

| Part type | Ceiling |
|---|---|
| Tyre or tube | 40 pounds |
| Wheelset | 250 pounds |

## Lead times

Tell the rider that a special order takes between 2 and 4 weeks. Chase the supplier if a part is more than 5 working days late. Never promise a delivery date earlier than the supplier's date.

## Stock

Order a spare chain or cable only if the stock is below two.

## Examples

A request for 3 inner tubes of size 700c is a small order. The parts catalogue lists 4200 items.

Supplier codes look like SUP-0457. A new inner tube costs 6 pounds.

# Loan bikes

A loan bike is lent while the rider's own bike is in the workshop. Each workshop keeps 6 loan bikes on its racks.

## Who may borrow

The rider's height must be between 150 and 195 centimetres.

## Loan length

The longest loan depends on the season, and the figure for summer is in the second item of the list below.

- In winter a loan may last up to 7 days
- In summer a loan may last up to 14 days

A loan may be extended once.

## Deposits

| Bike | Most deposit held |
|---|---|
| City bike | 50 pounds |
| Cargo bike | 150 pounds |
| Electric bike | 300 pounds |

## Late returns

Charge a late fee only after the bike is 2 days late. The late fee is 5 pounds a day. Never charge more than 25 pounds in late fees.

From a third late return on, the rider may not borrow again.

## Examples

Loan bikes are numbered LB-01 to LB-18. The loan scheme began in 2017.

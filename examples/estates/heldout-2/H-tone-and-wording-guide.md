# Tone and wording

## Length

Keep a chat message under 70 words. Write a subject line of 4 to 9 words.

## Style

Use one emoji at most, and none in a safety message. Do not use more than three exclamation marks in a message. Write numbers below eleven in words and larger numbers in digits.

Greet the rider by name in the first line. Two phrases to avoid are "as per" and "kindly revert".

## Examples

"Hi Priya, your bike will be ready on Thursday at 4." is a good first line. The style guide was last revised in June 2023.

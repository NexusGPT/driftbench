# Repair booking

There are three kinds of repair: a puncture fix, a standard service and a full overhaul. Book the shortest slot that fits.

## Time on the stand

| Repair | Time on the stand |
|---|---|
| Puncture fix | At least 20 minutes |
| Standard service | At least 90 minutes |
| Full overhaul | At least 4 hours |

## Booking rules

Book no further ahead than 10 weeks. Book the first service of a new bike for between 60 and 90 minutes.

A rider may move a booking once without a fee. A second move is charged a fee of 5 pounds.

A cancellation less than 12 hours before a slot is logged as a late cancellation. After four late cancellations in a year, tell the shop manager.

The number of bikes one rider may have in the workshop at once is set two sentences further on. A bike counts as in the workshop from the moment it is checked in. Four bikes is the most for one rider.

How long a finished bike may wait to be collected before a storage fee starts is given in the note at the foot of this file.

## Examples

A message such as "Can I bring the red tourer in at 4 pm on 9 June?" is a booking request. Answer with free slots and ask which one suits.

A standard service costs 85 pounds. The repair form is at version 3.

Note: the storage fee starts after 21 days.

# Memory and handoff notes

## What a note holds

A note holds four items: the workshop code, the rider's name, the bike and the next step. The note template is called handoff-v3.

## Size and age

Write at most 6 lines in a handoff note. A handoff note older than 72 hours is stale; ask the rider again. Never copy more than 150 characters of a rider's message into a note.

Archive a closed case note after 30 days and delete it after 120 days. Read at least the last 3 notes before you answer a returning rider.

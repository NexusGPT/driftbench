# Prescription refills

Refill requests go to a nurse. You never approve one yourself.

Check three things before you pass a request on:

- The pet was seen in the last 12 months
- The prescription has repeats left
- The owner gave the weight of the pet in kilograms, between 0.5 and 80

A prescription has at most 2 repeats. The number of repeats allowed for a chronic medicine is given in the next sentence.

Chronic medicines may have up to 6 repeats.

Do not pass on a request for a controlled medicine. Tell the owner to call the clinic.

A refill is ready within 2 working days. A request for more than a 30-day supply is declined. A refill that costs over 120 euros needs the owner's written consent.

## Examples

A request such as "Max is 12 kilograms and needs 25 mg tablets" is a refill request. The pharmacy's code is PH-77.

Dr Osei signed the refill policy in 2021. The refill form is at version 4.

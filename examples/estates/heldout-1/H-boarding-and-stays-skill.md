# Boarding and stays

The kennel manager confirms every stay. You collect the request.

## Length of stay

The longest stay is set in the next sentence.

A stay may last at most 14 nights.

Do not accept a stay of fewer than 2 nights over a public holiday.

## Kennels

| Size of pet | Most boarders at one time |
|---|---|
| Small | 10 |
| Large | 4 |

Isolation kennels are in room 4 and are never booked by you.

## Before arrival

A pet must have had its vaccination at least 14 days before it arrives. Allow up to 3 kilograms of food from the owner. Ask for a deposit of at least 20 percent of the stay price.

Each dog is walked at least twice a day.

We board dogs, cats and rabbits. The kennel block was rebuilt in 2018. The boarding form is at version 2.

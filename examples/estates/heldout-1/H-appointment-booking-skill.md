# Appointment booking

There are three kinds of visit: a check-up, a vaccination and a sick visit. Book the shortest slot that fits.

## Slot lengths

| Visit | Slot |
|---|---|
| Vaccination | At least 15 minutes |
| Sick visit | At least 30 minutes |
| Dental scale and polish | At least 60 minutes |

## Pets in one booking

| Booking type | Most pets |
|---|---|
| Single visit | Two |
| Group vaccination day | Six |

## Booking rules

Book no further ahead than 12 weeks. Book a new patient's first visit for between 30 and 45 minutes.

An owner may move a visit twice. A third move needs a nurse's approval. Moves must be asked for at least 24 hours before the visit.

A cancellation less than 4 hours before a visit is logged as a late cancellation. After three late cancellations in a year, tell the practice manager.

Send the reminder at least 2 days before the visit. Send a single reminder and no second one.

## Examples

A message such as "Can Bella come at 3 pm on 14 May?" is a booking request. Answer with free slots and ask which one suits.

The booking form is at version 3. Slot ids look like S-2231.

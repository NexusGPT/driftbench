# Messaging and tone

Short is kind. Owners read on a phone.

## Length

Keep a chat message under 60 words. Keep an email under 150 words.

## Style

Use one emoji at most, and none in a complaint. Do not use more than two exclamation marks in a message.

Spell out numbers under 10 and write the others as digits.

Three words to avoid are "unfortunately", "policy" and "regulations".

## Example

"Hello Mara, Biscuit's visit is on Tuesday at 10." is a good first line. The style guide was last revised in March 2022.

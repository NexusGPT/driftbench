# Complaints and refunds

Log every complaint with a code. Codes look like C-2024-118.

## Time limits

Acknowledge a complaint within 1 hour during opening times. Answer it in full within 5 working days.

If a complaint is not solved in 10 working days, pass it to the practice manager.

## Goodwill

For a missed appointment you may offer between 10 and 25 euros. Never offer a goodwill credit for a complaint older than 90 days.

## Tone

Apologise once per message. If the owner writes three times about the same problem, pass the case to a nurse.

Last year the clinics logged 212 complaints. Most were about waiting times.

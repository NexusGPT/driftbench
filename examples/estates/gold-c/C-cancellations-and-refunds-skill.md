# Cancellations and refunds

Cancellations are common in autumn when the weather turns. The rules are fixed and the front desk applies them. You explain them and open the case.

## The cancellation rules

| Time before arrival | Refund |
|---|---|
| More than fourteen days | Everything except the booking fee |
| Seven to fourteen days | Half of the stay |
| Less than seven days | Nothing, unless the room is re-let |

State the rule that fits the guest's dates and no other. Do not read the whole table aloud.

You never decide a refund. The front desk decides and sends the confirmation.

## First reply

Say you are sorry for the change of plans, in one short sentence. Then say what the rule gives for their dates.

If the guest says the cancellation is for illness or a death in the family, do not quote the table. Say the front desk will look at the case kindly and pass it on.

## Opening the case

Write the case note with the booking reference, the dates and the reason. Add the rule that applies.

Tell the guest that the front desk replies within two working days. Never promise a faster answer.

## What each channel changes

| Channel | Tell the guest |
|---|---|
| WhatsApp | The rule in one line and the reply time |
| Email | The rule, the reply time and the case number |
| Phone | The rule once, then offer to send it by text |
| Web chat | The rule and a link to the cancellation page |

## When the guest is upset

An upset guest wants to be heard before they want to be told a rule. Answer the worry, not the tone.

Do not argue with a guest about the rule. Do not say the rule is unfair, and do not say it is fair. Say what it is and who can look again.

If the guest asks to speak to the owner, pass the conversation on at once. Write a note that says the guest asked for the owner.

## Gift vouchers

A booking paid with a gift voucher is refunded as a new voucher and not as money. Explain this before the guest asks for cash.

## Records

Every cancellation gets a case, even when the refund is nothing. The owners read the cases at the end of each month.

Remember that a kind answer to a cancelling guest is the best advertisement for the next booking.

That is all.

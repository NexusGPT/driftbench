# Booking skill

This skill holds the steps for a booking and how each channel changes them. The booking calendar is the only source of availability.

## The steps

Follow the steps in this order:

1. Ask for the dates and the number of guests
2. Check the calendar for free rooms
3. Offer one or two rooms with the price
4. Hold the room when the guest agrees
5. Ask for the details needed to confirm

Do not hold a room before the guest has agreed to it. A hold blocks the room for twenty minutes.

## Rooms

| Room | Sleeps | Price per night |
|---|---|---|
| Sea Room | Two | 92 pounds |
| Garden Room | Two | 84 pounds |
| Loft | Four | 128 pounds |
| Dune Cottage | Five | 160 pounds |

Offer the cheapest room that fits the party. Mention a dearer room only if the guest asks for more space.

## Minimum stays

Weekends from May to September need a minimum stay of two nights. If the guest asks for one night, offer the nearest weekday night instead. Do not bend the minimum stay.

## Details to confirm

Ask for the full name, a phone number and the expected arrival time. Say that rooms are ready from three in the afternoon.

Do not ask for a card number in the chat. The front desk takes payment by phone.

## What each channel changes

| Channel | Do |
|---|---|
| WhatsApp | Offer one room, not two, and ask a yes or no question |
| Email | Offer two rooms and attach the confirmation pdf |
| Phone | Offer one room, say the price once and repeat the dates at the end |
| Web chat | Offer two rooms with the free dates shown |

## Changing a booking

A guest may move a stay up to seven days before arrival without a charge. Inside seven days a change counts as a new booking.

Check the new dates in the calendar before you cancel the old ones. Never cancel a booking first and look for a new room afterwards.

Confirm the change in writing on the channel the guest used. Give the new dates and the new price.

## Group bookings

A group is more than eight guests or more than three rooms. Groups are not booked by you. Pass the request to the front desk with the number of guests and the dates.

## Special requests

Guests ask for dogs, early arrival, a cot, or a quiet room. Dogs are allowed in the Garden Room and the Dune Cottage only. Say yes only when the room allows it. Add a note to the booking for every request.

If a request is not in the price list or the room notes, do not promise it. Say you will ask the front desk and pass the request on.

Example: "The Garden Room welcomes dogs. I have added a note for your dog to the booking, and the front desk will confirm the dog charge on arrival."

## Before you finish

Before you end the conversation, check that:

- The dates are repeated back to the guest
- The price and the room are stated
- The guest knows how payment works

Wish the guest a good trip.

Thanks.

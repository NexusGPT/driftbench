# Complaints

A complaint is any message in which a guest says something went wrong. Most are small and a fast, honest answer ends them.

## Everywhere

Thank the guest for telling us. Do not start with an excuse.

Repeat the problem in your own words, in one sentence, so the guest knows you understood. Then say what you will do.

Never blame a member of staff by name. Never say the problem is the guest's fault.

Offer no compensation yourself. The owners decide whether a guest receives a refund, a voucher or an apology letter.

Write a case note for every complaint, even a small one. Include the guest, the room, the dates and the problem.

## Tab: WhatsApp

**Applies when the complaint arrives on WhatsApp.** The guest is often still in the room.

Reply at once with one line that says you are on it. Then ask one question that helps the front desk, such as which room.

If the guest sends a photo, say thank you and pass the photo on. Do not comment on what the photo shows.

Ask the guest whether the front desk may come to the room now. Wait for the answer before you send anyone.

## Tab: Email

**Applies when the complaint arrives by email.** The guest has had time to think and may be angry.

Answer within one working day. Open with thanks, name the problem and say who will look into it.

Do not copy the whole complaint back to the guest. Do not write more than ten lines.

Close with the name of the person who will reply and the day they will do it. If the front desk has not given a day, write "within two working days".

## Tab: Phone

**Applies when the guest complains on the phone.** The guest wants to be heard.

Let the guest finish before you speak. Do not interrupt, even to agree.

Say sorry once, slowly. Say what happens next in one sentence.

If the guest raises their voice, keep your voice level and do not match theirs. If they ask for the owner, say you will fetch them and do it.

Offer a call back from the owner if the owner is not at the desk. Never leave a phone complaint without a promised call back time.

## Tab: Web chat

**Applies when the complaint arrives in the chat window.** The guest may not yet have booked.

Answer the question in the complaint first. If the complaint is about a past stay, ask for the dates and the room.

Do not argue about reviews. Say that you will pass the comment to the owners.

If the guest threatens to post a bad review, do not plead and do not warn. Thank them for the feedback and carry on helping.

## What counts as urgent

| Problem | Tell | Within |
|---|---|---|
| No heating or hot water | The duty owner | Ten minutes |
| A lock that will not open | The duty owner | Five minutes |
| Noise from another room | The front desk | Thirty minutes |
| A stain or a dirty room | The front desk | One hour |

Use the times in the table when you tell the guest what to expect. Do not shorten them.

## After the complaint

When the owners have decided, the case note shows the decision. Read it before you write to the guest again.

Tell the guest the decision in plain words. If it is a refund, say how much and when it will arrive.

Remember that a guest who complained and was well handled often returns.

Thanks for your patience.

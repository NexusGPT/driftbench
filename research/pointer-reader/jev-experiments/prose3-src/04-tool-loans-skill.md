# Tool loans{{n}}

The café owns about forty hand tools and power tools, and members may borrow them for a week.{{d}} A membership costs six pounds a year and is paid at the door.{{d}}

Do not lend a tool to a visitor who is not a member.{{p}}

## Lending{{n}}

Look the member up in the member list before you lend anything.{{g}}

Write four things in the loan book:{{r#a}}

- The member's name{{r#a}}
- The tool and its number{{r#a}}
- The date of the loan{{r#a}}
- The date it is due back{{r#a}}

A member asks for a power tool.{{g#b}} Check the date of their safety sheet, and if it is older than a year, ask them to sign a new one before the loan.{{g#b}}

Never lend the angle grinder.{{p#c}} The one exception is a member with the grinder badge, and then only on the day of a session.{{p#c}}

Lend no more than two tools to one member at a time.{{p}}

The tool number is on a yellow tag, and the tag stays on the tool.{{d}}

## Taking tools back{{n}}

Check each tool when it comes back.{{r#d}} Test the switch, wipe the blade and count the pieces against the list on the lid, then write checked and your initials on the loan line.{{r#d}}

A tool comes back damaged.{{r#e}} Take a photograph, tell the session lead, and mark the tool out of service in the book.{{r#e}}

Tell the member to bring three things to the desk on the day a tool goes back:{{r#f}}

- The tool itself, with its cord{{r#f}}
- The yellow tag, if it has come off{{r#f}}
- The loan slip they were given{{r#f}}

## Late returns{{n}}

The loan book shows which loans are late each Saturday morning.{{d}} The session lead reads the list out at the start of the session.{{d}}

| Days late{{n}} | Then{{n}} |
|---|---|
| One to seven{{r#t1}} | Send a reminder and charge nothing{{r#t1}} |
| Eight to fourteen{{r#t2}} | Charge one pound a day and say so in the message{{r#t2}} |
| More than fourteen{{r#t3}} | Pass the loan to the session lead{{r#t3}} |

Keep reminders polite and short.{{r#g}} Name the tool, name the due date and ask when the member can bring it back.{{r#g}}

Example: "Hello Sam, the cordless drill, tool 14, was due back on the 3rd.{{e}} Could you bring it on Saturday, or tell me if you need another week?"{{e}}

## Lost and broken{{n}}

If a tool is lost, the member pays half the price of a new one.{{d}} The session lead decides the sum and writes it in the loan book.{{d}}

Do not quote a sum to the member yourself.{{p}}

A member says a tool was already broken when they borrowed it.{{p#h}} Do not argue.{{p#h}} Look up the check line from the last return, and pass both to the session lead.{{p#h}}

## Tool care{{n}}

Power tools are kept in the locked cupboard and hand tools on the open rack.{{d}} The cupboard key hangs in the porch beside the breaker key.{{d}}

Keep blades dry and cords coiled loosely.{{r}} A tight coil cracks the insulation over time.{{d}}

Never leave a tool on a bench overnight.{{p#i}} Put it back on the rack, or in the cupboard if it is a power tool.{{p#i}}

The tool cupboard is checked by the session lead on the last Saturday of the month.{{d}} Any tool missing from the list is written up and the member list is searched.{{d}}

That is all for tool loans.{{n}}

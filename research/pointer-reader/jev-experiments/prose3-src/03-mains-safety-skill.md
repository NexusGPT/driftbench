# Mains safety{{n}}

Mains electricity can kill, and a repair café is a busy and friendly place.{{d}} The checks below stop the busy and friendly from getting in the way.{{d}}

## Before a bench is set up{{n}}

Before a mains item is opened, check all of these:{{g#a}}

- The plug is out of the wall{{g#a}}
- The cable has no cuts and no tape{{g#a}}
- The item has stood unplugged for ten minutes{{g#a}}
- The volunteer holds the mains badge{{g#a}}

If any check fails, the item does not go on a bench.{{r#b}} Give the visitor a note to take to a qualified electrician.{{r#b}}

Capacitors hold a charge after the plug is out.{{d}} Televisions, microwave ovens and camera flashes are the usual culprits.{{d}}

Never open a microwave oven, however kindly the visitor asks and however experienced the volunteer.{{p}}

The testing meter goes to mains-badge volunteers only; hand it to nobody else.{{p}}

## Signs that something is wrong{{n}}

| Sign{{n}} | What it means{{n}} | Then{{n}} |
|---|---|---|
| Burning smell{{r#t1}} | An overheated part{{r#t1}} | Unplug at once and carry the item outside{{r#t1}} |
| Warm cable{{p#t2}} | An overloaded lead{{p#t2}} | Stop, and do not reuse the cable{{p#t2}} |
| Tripped breaker{{p#t3}} | A fault somewhere on the bench{{p#t3}} | Reset it once only, and only the session lead resets it{{p#t3}} |

The breaker board is in the porch cupboard and the key hangs beside it.{{d}}

## If someone is hurt{{n}}

If a visitor or a volunteer is hurt, stop everything else at the bench.{{g#c}} Then send the first aider's name to the nearest volunteer and say where the person is.{{g#c}} Stay with the person until the first aider arrives.{{g#c}}

The first aider's number is on the lanyard board and on the café wall.{{d}}

Call the emergency number yourself if the person is not breathing.{{r}} Do not wait for the first aider to arrive.{{p}}

## Resetting and restarting{{n}}

Reset a tripped breaker once only.{{r#d}} If it trips again, leave it alone and call the session lead.{{r#d}}

Switch an item on only after the session lead has signed the ticket.{{g}} A signature in the wrong box does not count, and the lead will ask for it again.{{d}}

The fuse drawer holds fuses of 3, 5 and 13 amps.{{d}} Fit a fuse of the same rating as the one you take out.{{p#e}} Never fit a larger one to make a fault go away.{{p#e}} Larger fuses hide faults, and the faults come back.{{d}}

Case: in March a volunteer opened a camera flash and felt the charge in his thumb.{{e}} He was unhurt, and the café now keeps a discharge stick in the porch cupboard.{{e}}

## Training{{n}}

The mains badge is a one-hour test taken twice a year at the library.{{d}} Volunteers who fail may retake it after a month.{{d}} The list of badge holders is pinned beside the breaker board.{{d}}

Ask a volunteer to show their badge before you pass them a mains ticket.{{p#f}} Do not take their word for it, and do not accept a badge from last year.{{p#f}}

Stay safe.{{n}}

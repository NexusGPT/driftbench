# Complaints{{n}}

A complaint is any message in which a guest says something went wrong.{{d}} Most are small and a fast, honest answer ends them.{{d}}

## Everywhere{{n}}

Thank the guest for telling us.{{r}} Do not start with an excuse.{{p}}

Repeat the problem in your own words, in one sentence, so the guest knows you understood.{{r}} Then say what you will do.{{r}}

Never blame a member of staff by name.{{p}} Never say the problem is the guest's fault.{{p}}

Offer no compensation yourself.{{p}} The owners decide whether a guest receives a refund, a voucher or an apology letter.{{d}}

Write a case note for every complaint, even a small one.{{r#a}} Include the guest, the room, the dates and the problem.{{r#a}}

## Tab: WhatsApp{{n}}

**Applies when the complaint arrives on WhatsApp.**{{d}} The guest is often still in the room.{{d}}

Reply at once with one line that says you are on it.{{r}} Then ask one question that helps the front desk, such as which room.{{r}}

If the guest sends a photo, say thank you and pass the photo on.{{r}} Do not comment on what the photo shows.{{p}}

Ask the guest whether the front desk may come to the room now.{{r}} Wait for the answer before you send anyone.{{g}}

## Tab: Email{{n}}

**Applies when the complaint arrives by email.**{{d}} The guest has had time to think and may be angry.{{d}}

Answer within one working day.{{r}} Open with thanks, name the problem and say who will look into it.{{r}}

Do not copy the whole complaint back to the guest.{{p}} Do not write more than ten lines.{{p}}

Close with the name of the person who will reply and the day they will do it.{{r}} If the front desk has not given a day, write "within two working days".{{r}}

## Tab: Phone{{n}}

**Applies when the guest complains on the phone.**{{d}} The guest wants to be heard.{{d}}

Let the guest finish before you speak.{{g}} Do not interrupt, even to agree.{{p}}

Say sorry once, slowly.{{r}} Say what happens next in one sentence.{{r}}

If the guest raises their voice, keep your voice level and do not match theirs.{{r}} If they ask for the owner, say you will fetch them and do it.{{r}}

Offer a call back from the owner if the owner is not at the desk.{{r}} Never leave a phone complaint without a promised call back time.{{p}}

## Tab: Web chat{{n}}

**Applies when the complaint arrives in the chat window.**{{d}} The guest may not yet have booked.{{d}}

Answer the question in the complaint first.{{r}} If the complaint is about a past stay, ask for the dates and the room.{{r}}

Do not argue about reviews.{{p}} Say that you will pass the comment to the owners.{{r}}

If the guest threatens to post a bad review, do not plead and do not warn.{{p}} Thank them for the feedback and carry on helping.{{r}}

## What counts as urgent{{n}}

| Problem{{n}} | Tell{{n}} | Within{{n}} |
|---|---|---|
| No heating or hot water{{d}} | The duty owner{{d}} | Ten minutes{{d}} |
| A lock that will not open{{d}} | The duty owner{{d}} | Five minutes{{d}} |
| Noise from another room{{d}} | The front desk{{d}} | Thirty minutes{{d}} |
| A stain or a dirty room{{d}} | The front desk{{d}} | One hour{{d}} |

Use the times in the table when you tell the guest what to expect.{{r}} Do not shorten them.{{p}}

## After the complaint{{n}}

When the owners have decided, the case note shows the decision.{{d}} Read it before you write to the guest again.{{g}}

Tell the guest the decision in plain words.{{r}} If it is a refund, say how much and when it will arrive.{{r}}

Remember that a guest who complained and was well handled often returns.{{d}}

Thanks for your patience.{{n}}

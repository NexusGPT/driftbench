# Equipment returns{{n}}

Quillfisher lends a router to every customer and a set-top box to some.{{d}} The equipment stays ours and must come back when the customer leaves.{{d}}

## What counts as equipment{{n}}

The list is short:{{d}}

- The router{{d}}
- The power adapter of the router{{d}}
- The set-top box and its remote{{d}}
- The white wall socket cover, if it was fitted by us{{d}}

Cables that the customer bought themselves are theirs to keep.{{d}}

## Starting a return{{n}}

Check that the customer has told us they are leaving or upgrading.{{g}} Never start a return because a customer sounds unhappy.{{p}}

Ask which pieces of equipment they still have at home.{{r}} Write them down one by one in the ticket.{{r}}

Offer the free return label first.{{r#a}} If the customer cannot print it, offer a drop-off at the post office on Mill Lane instead.{{r#a}}

## The return label{{n}}

The label is sent by email and is valid for thirty days.{{d}} It can be used once.{{d}}

Tell the customer to pack the equipment in the original box if they have it.{{r}} If they have no box, any sturdy box will do.{{d}}

Remind them to unplug the router only after the new connection works.{{g}} A customer who unplugs early loses their service for the gap.{{d}}

## What to do for each situation{{n}}

| Situation{{n}} | Do{{n}} |
|---|---|
| The customer has the original box{{r}} | Send the label and say the box is enough{{r}} |
| The customer lost the power adapter{{r}} | Note it on the ticket and say a fee may apply{{r}} |
| The customer lost the router{{p}} | Do not promise to waive the fee; send the ticket to billing{{p}} |
| The router is damaged{{r}} | Ask for a photo and attach it to the ticket{{r}} |

## Charges{{n}}

An unreturned router is charged at forty pounds after sixty days.{{d}} A set-top box is charged at sixty pounds.{{d}} The charge is added to the final bill.{{d}}

Do not quote these amounts as a threat.{{p}} Mention them only if the customer asks what happens when equipment is lost.{{r}}

You cannot waive a charge.{{d}} Only the billing team can.{{d}}

## Tracking a return{{n}}

Once the parcel is posted, the tracking number appears on the ticket within two days.{{d}} If the customer asks where it is, look it up.{{r}} Do not tell them it is lost until five working days have passed.{{p}}

When the warehouse scans the parcel, the ticket closes by itself.{{d}} You do not close return tickets by hand.{{p}}

## A note on tone{{n}}

A customer who is leaving is still a customer.{{d}} Thank them for their time with us, and never try to talk them out of leaving.{{p}}

If they ask why we want the router back, say plainly that it is ours and that we reuse it.{{r}}

## Checks before you finish{{n}}

Before you end the conversation, make sure that:{{g#b}}

- The customer has a label or a drop-off address{{g#b}}
- The ticket lists every piece of equipment{{g#b}}
- You told them the thirty-day limit{{g#b}}

Wish them well.{{r}}

Thank you.{{n}}

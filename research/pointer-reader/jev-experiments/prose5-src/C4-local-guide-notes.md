# Local guide notes{{n}}

These notes are facts, not instructions.{{d}} Share them when a guest asks, and only then.{{r}}

## Getting here{{n}}

The nearest station is Saltby, eleven minutes from the Sea Room by taxi.{{d}} There are four trains a day and the last one leaves at nine in the evening.{{d}}

The village bus runs on weekdays only.{{d}} A guest who arrives on a Sunday needs a taxi.{{d}}

If a guest asks for a taxi number, give the one on the staff sheet.{{r}} Do not recommend a different firm.{{p}}

## Eating{{n}}

The Anchor serves food until eight and is closed on Mondays.{{d}} The harbour kiosk sells fish and chips from noon until the light goes.{{d}} The farm shop sells picnic boxes if they are ordered the evening before.{{d}}

Breakfast is served between half past seven and half past nine.{{d}} A guest who wants it earlier can ask the front desk.{{r}}

## Walking{{n}}

The coast path runs past all three guesthouses.{{d}} The stretch to the lighthouse takes about two hours and has no shelter.{{d}}

Remind walkers to check the tide table before they set out along the sands.{{r}} The tide comes in fast near the point.{{d}}

Do not suggest a route to a guest who says they are not used to walking.{{p}} Offer the shorter loop through the village instead.{{r}}

## Weather{{n}}

Winds are strong in autumn and spring.{{d}} A guest who asks about the weather should be told to check the forecast on the day.{{r}} Never promise good weather.{{p}}

## Beaches and dogs{{n}}

Dogs are allowed on the east beach all year.{{d}} On the west beach they are banned from May to September.{{d}}

Tell a guest with a dog about the west beach rule.{{r}} Many do not know it.{{d}}

## Events{{n}}

The harbour has a small market on the first Saturday of each month.{{d}} The lifeboat open day is in August.{{d}} Dates change, so say "usually" and send the guest to the village page for the exact day.{{r}}

Example: "The market is usually on the first Saturday of the month.{{e}} The village page has the date for your stay."{{e}}

## Emergencies in the area{{n}}

The coastguard number is on the staff sheet.{{d}} If a walker is in trouble on the cliffs, tell the guest to call 999 and ask for the coastguard.{{r}}

Do not give directions to the rescue point on the cliffs.{{p}} The emergency services use their own.{{d}}

Enjoy the coast.{{n}}

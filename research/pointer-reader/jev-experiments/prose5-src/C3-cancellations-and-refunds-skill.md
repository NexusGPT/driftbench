# Cancellations and refunds{{n}}

Cancellations are common in autumn when the weather turns.{{d}} The rules are fixed and the front desk applies them.{{d}} You explain them and open the case.{{d}}

## The cancellation rules{{n}}

| Time before arrival{{n}} | Refund{{n}} |
|---|---|
| More than fourteen days{{d}} | Everything except the booking fee{{d}} |
| Seven to fourteen days{{d}} | Half of the stay{{d}} |
| Less than seven days{{d}} | Nothing, unless the room is re-let{{d}} |

State the rule that fits the guest's dates and no other.{{r}} Do not read the whole table aloud.{{p}}

You never decide a refund.{{p}} The front desk decides and sends the confirmation.{{d}}

## First reply{{n}}

Say you are sorry for the change of plans, in one short sentence.{{r}} Then say what the rule gives for their dates.{{r}}

If the guest says the cancellation is for illness or a death in the family, do not quote the table.{{p}} Say the front desk will look at the case kindly and pass it on.{{r}}

## Opening the case{{n}}

Write the case note with the booking reference, the dates and the reason.{{r#a}} Add the rule that applies.{{r#a}}

Tell the guest that the front desk replies within two working days.{{r}} Never promise a faster answer.{{p}}

## What each channel changes{{n}}

| Channel{{n}} | Tell the guest{{n}} |
|---|---|
| WhatsApp{{d}} | The rule in one line and the reply time{{r}} |
| Email{{d}} | The rule, the reply time and the case number{{r}} |
| Phone{{d}} | The rule once, then offer to send it by text{{r}} |
| Web chat{{d}} | The rule and a link to the cancellation page{{r}} |

## When the guest is upset{{n}}

An upset guest wants to be heard before they want to be told a rule.{{d}} Answer the worry, not the tone.{{r}}

Do not argue with a guest about the rule.{{p}} Do not say the rule is unfair, and do not say it is fair.{{p}} Say what it is and who can look again.{{r}}

If the guest asks to speak to the owner, pass the conversation on at once.{{r#b}} Write a note that says the guest asked for the owner.{{r#b}}

## Gift vouchers{{n}}

A booking paid with a gift voucher is refunded as a new voucher and not as money.{{d}} Explain this before the guest asks for cash.{{r}}

## Records{{n}}

Every cancellation gets a case, even when the refund is nothing.{{r}} The owners read the cases at the end of each month.{{d}}

Remember that a kind answer to a cancelling guest is the best advertisement for the next booking.{{d}}

That is all.{{n}}

# Booking skill{{n}}

This skill holds the steps for a booking and how each channel changes them.{{d}} The booking calendar is the only source of availability.{{d}}

## The steps{{n}}

Follow the steps in this order:{{g#a}}

1. Ask for the dates and the number of guests{{g#a}}
2. Check the calendar for free rooms{{g#a}}
3. Offer one or two rooms with the price{{g#a}}
4. Hold the room when the guest agrees{{g#a}}
5. Ask for the details needed to confirm{{g#a}}

Do not hold a room before the guest has agreed to it.{{p}} A hold blocks the room for twenty minutes.{{d}}

## Rooms{{n}}

| Room{{n}} | Sleeps{{n}} | Price per night{{n}} |
|---|---|---|
| Sea Room{{d}} | Two{{d}} | 92 pounds{{d}} |
| Garden Room{{d}} | Two{{d}} | 84 pounds{{d}} |
| Loft{{d}} | Four{{d}} | 128 pounds{{d}} |
| Dune Cottage{{d}} | Five{{d}} | 160 pounds{{d}} |

Offer the cheapest room that fits the party.{{r}} Mention a dearer room only if the guest asks for more space.{{r}}

## Minimum stays{{n}}

Weekends from May to September need a minimum stay of two nights.{{d}} If the guest asks for one night, offer the nearest weekday night instead.{{r}} Do not bend the minimum stay.{{p}}

## Details to confirm{{n}}

Ask for the full name, a phone number and the expected arrival time.{{r#b}} Say that rooms are ready from three in the afternoon.{{r#b}}

Do not ask for a card number in the chat.{{p}} The front desk takes payment by phone.{{d}}

## What each channel changes{{n}}

| Channel{{n}} | Do{{n}} |
|---|---|
| WhatsApp{{d}} | Offer one room, not two, and ask a yes or no question{{r}} |
| Email{{d}} | Offer two rooms and attach the confirmation pdf{{r}} |
| Phone{{d}} | Offer one room, say the price once and repeat the dates at the end{{r}} |
| Web chat{{d}} | Offer two rooms with the free dates shown{{r}} |

## Changing a booking{{n}}

A guest may move a stay up to seven days before arrival without a charge.{{d}} Inside seven days a change counts as a new booking.{{d}}

Check the new dates in the calendar before you cancel the old ones.{{g}} Never cancel a booking first and look for a new room afterwards.{{p}}

Confirm the change in writing on the channel the guest used.{{r}} Give the new dates and the new price.{{r}}

## Group bookings{{n}}

A group is more than eight guests or more than three rooms.{{d}} Groups are not booked by you.{{d}} Pass the request to the front desk with the number of guests and the dates.{{r}}

## Special requests{{n}}

Guests ask for dogs, early arrival, a cot, or a quiet room.{{d}} Dogs are allowed in the Garden Room and the Dune Cottage only.{{d}} Say yes only when the room allows it.{{r}} Add a note to the booking for every request.{{r}}

If a request is not in the price list or the room notes, do not promise it.{{p}} Say you will ask the front desk and pass the request on.{{r}}

Example: "The Garden Room welcomes dogs.{{e}} I have added a note for your dog to the booking, and the front desk will confirm the dog charge on arrival."{{e}}

## Before you finish{{n}}

Before you end the conversation, check that:{{g#c}}

- The dates are repeated back to the guest{{g#c}}
- The price and the room are stated{{g#c}}
- The guest knows how payment works{{g#c}}

Wish the guest a good trip.{{r}}

Thanks.{{n}}

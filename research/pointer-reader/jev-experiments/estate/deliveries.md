# Deliveries

Deliveries arrive between six and eight on Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday. The flour driver is called Odalys and the butter driver is called Fenn.

## Receiving

Count the sacks at the back door before you sign the driver's sheet. Check the date on each butter block. Sign the driver's sheet only after the count matches the sheet. Never let a driver leave sacks outside the back door. For example, a sack of tarn flour left in the rain on a Saturday had to be thrown away.

## Storing

- Put new flour sacks behind the old ones in the flour store
- Stand butter blocks in the cold room on the middle shelf
- Do not stack flour sacks higher than four
- The flour store is the dry room next to the back door
- A stack of five sacks on the left wall tipped over last winter and split three of them

## Problems

Phone the supplier if a sack is torn or a butter block is soft. Take a photo of a damaged sack before you move it. Do not use flour from a torn sack in the dough. A torn sack may let in damp and insects. Case: flour from a torn sack went into a batch on a Thursday and the whole batch was binned.

## Paperwork

Hand the signed sheet to the shop manager by noon. The shop manager files each sheet in the grey box. Greet the drivers with a smile.

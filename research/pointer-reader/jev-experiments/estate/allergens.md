# Allergens

Nuts, milk, egg, sesame and gluten are the five allergens that appear in our products. Gluten appears in every loaf, so the gluten line is printed on every label.

## Labels

Print the allergen label for every new product before it goes on a shelf. Read the recipe card to the head baker before you print a new label. Never guess an allergen from the name of a product. For example, a poppy roll can still carry sesame if it shares a tray with sesame buns. Put the label on the front of the sack, not on the back.

## Answering customers

- Say plainly which allergens a product contains
- Offer the allergen folder if the customer asks for more detail
- Do not say that anything is free of nuts
- The allergen folder sits on the counter by the till

## Cross contact

The walnut loaf is baked on its own tray in the small oven. Wash the walnut tray in its own sink with its own cloth. Do not bake a walnut loaf and a sesame bun in the same oven load. Wipe the small oven deck clean before the walnut loaf goes in. Case: a nut free customer reacted to a bun that was baked after the walnut loaf on a dirty deck.

## Emergencies

Call the emergency number first if a customer reacts to food. Tell the shop manager after the call. The emergency number is written on the wall beside the sconce room door. Stay calm and speak clearly.

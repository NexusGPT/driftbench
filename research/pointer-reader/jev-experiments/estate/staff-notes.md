# Staff notes

The crew has eleven people and three shifts: opening, middle and closing. Welcome to Crumbwick.

## Shifts

| Shift | Starts | Ends |
|---|---|---|
| Opening | Half past five | Half past one |
| Middle | Nine | Five |
| Closing | Noon | Half past six |

Phone the shop manager at least one hour before the shift if you will be late. Do not swap a shift without the shop manager's written note. Write the swap in the shift book before you tell the other person.

## Breaks

- Take your break away from the ovens and the loft
- Wash your hands before you return to the shelves
- Do not eat on the shop floor
- The break room is the small room at the end of the back corridor
- A crew member who ate a roll behind the till left crumbs that a customer later complained about

## Uniform

Wear the white apron and the grey cap in the loft and at the ovens. Do not wear rings in the loft. Aprons are washed on Sunday by the closing crew. Case: a ring dropped into a trough once and the whole batch of dough had to be searched.

## Asking for help

Ask the head baker if you do not know a step. Write a question in the question book if the head baker is away. Read the question book at the start of each shift. The question book lies on the table in the break room. Nobody minds a good question.

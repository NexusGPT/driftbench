# Closing the shop

Closing takes about forty minutes and starts when the last customer has left. Turn the street door sign to closed as soon as the last customer leaves.

## The ovens and lamps

Switch off both ovens before you switch off the lamps. Never leave an oven on overnight, even at the lowest setting. Pull the oven racks out and stand them upright on the cooling plinth. The cooling plinth is the long steel bench under the east window. Switch off the front lamps only after the oven check is written in the closing book. For instance, a warm oven at closing means the check goes in the book with a note.

## The shelves

- Move unsold loaves into the paper sacks on the left shelf
- Label each sack with the day and the kind of loaf
- Do not put cream cakes in the sacks, because they spoil by morning
- Unsold sacks go to the food bank on Tuesday and Friday
- A sack of six rye loaves marked Thursday goes out on Friday

## The tills

Count the till drawer twice before you lock it in the sconce room. Write the total in the closing book and sign it. Do not carry the cash bag out of the shop at night. The closing book lives on the hook beside the sconce room door.

## The door

Return every key to the sconce room before you lock the street door. Lock the street door with two turns of the brass key. The alarm panel gives you ninety seconds to leave once it is set. Thank you for closing carefully.

# Ovens

The big oven is a deck oven with three decks, and the small oven is a single deck. Both ovens run on gas, and the gas tap sits behind the flour store.

## Lighting

Open the gas tap before you press the spark button. Never press the spark button more than three times in a row. If the flame does not catch after three tries, close the tap and wait five minutes. Example: on a windy morning the pilot flame often blows out twice before it holds. Call the gas engineer if the flame turns yellow instead of blue.

## Daily care

- Brush the oven floor with the long brass brush after the last tray
- Wipe the oven door glass with the dry grey cloth
- Do not spray water on a hot deck
- Do not use the steel scraper on the door glass
- The grey cloth hangs on the hook beside the big oven

## Weekly care

Soak the racks in the sink with warm water and the green soap every Sunday. Let the ovens cool completely before you soak the racks. A full soak takes about an hour and needs two people. Case: one crew soaked a hot rack and the steel warped so badly that it had to be replaced.

## Faults

| Sign | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Yellow flame | The gas mix is wrong | Close the tap and call the engineer |
| Clicking with no flame | The spark button is damp | Dry the button with the grey cloth |
| Smell of gas | A leak somewhere in the line | Open the doors and leave the shop |

Never switch on a lamp or the till if you smell gas. Ask the opening crew if a fault looks new.

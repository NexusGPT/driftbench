# Tills

There are two tills, one at the street door and one at the pickup shelf. Each till has its own drawer key, and the keys live in the sconce room.

## Opening a till

Count the float in the drawer before you log in. Log in with your own crew number. Never share a crew number with another person. For example, a shared number made it impossible to find who had given a wrong change on a Saturday. The float is eighty pounds in coins and notes.

## Taking payment

- Say the total aloud before the customer pays
- Offer a receipt with every sale
- Do not open the drawer without a sale
- Do not write a card number on paper
- The card reader sits on the left of each till

## Mistakes

Call the shop manager to void a sale, and wait for the manager to key the void. Keep the receipt of the wrong sale before the manager voids it. Never void your own sale. Case: a crew member voided a sale of two loaves and kept the cash, and the count was short by that amount. The shop manager holds the void key on a red cord.

## Counting

Count the drawer twice at closing and write both counts in the book. Ask a second person to count it if the two counts differ. A difference of more than two pounds is reported to the shop manager. Take your time with the count.

# Cleaning

The shop is cleaned every day and the loft and back rooms are cleaned every week. The cleaning cupboard is the tall green cupboard in the back corridor.

## Daily

- Sweep the shop floor from the walls toward the door
- Mop the floor with the blue bucket
- Empty the crumb tray under each shelf
- Do not use the blue bucket in the loft
- The blue bucket is marked with a blue strip of tape

## Surfaces

Wipe the counter and the till with the yellow cloth. Wipe the counter before you wipe the till. Never use the yellow cloth on the floor. For example, a yellow cloth used on the floor was found on the counter the next morning, grey with grit.

## Weekly

| Day | Area | Who |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | The loft | Opening crew |
| Wednesday | The cold room | Closing crew |
| Friday | The back corridor | Head baker |

Clear the cold room shelves completely before you wash them on Wednesday. Do not wash the cold room shelves with hot water. Unplug the cold room fan before you wipe its cover.

## Waste

Tie the waste bags with a double knot. Put the bags in the grey bins by the back door. Never put broken glass in a waste bag. Broken glass goes in the red tin beside the cleaning cupboard. Case: a bag with broken glass cut the hand of a collector last year. Cleaning is everyone's job.

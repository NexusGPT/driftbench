# Seasonal baking

Crumbwick bakes four seasonal loaves each year: spring dill, summer fig, autumn oat and winter spice. Seasonal loaves go on level two beside the pinnock loaves.

## Winter spice loaf

Bake the winter spice loaf in the big oven only. Do not add the spice mix until the dough has risen once. Let the dough rise once before you add the spice mix. For example, a spice mix added at the start made the dough sticky and the crumb grey. The spice mix has cinnamon, clove and a pinch of orrel seed.

## Summer fig loaf

- Soak the figs in warm water for ten minutes
- Dry the figs on a clean cloth
- Do not add wet figs to the dough
- Figs come in a box of sixty and arrive on Thursday
- A fig loaf with wet figs split along the top in the oven

## Orders for the season

Take Christmas orders from the first of November. Check the oven plan for the week before you accept a big Christmas order. Never accept a Christmas order after the twentieth of December. The oven plan hangs on the wall of the dough loft. Case: a late order for sixty winter spice loaves took the whole oven for a night and the daily bread came late.

## Decorations

Hang the paper stars in the window on the first of December. Do not tape anything to the glass of the oven door. The shop looks lovely in the snow.

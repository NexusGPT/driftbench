# Taking orders

Orders come in by phone, by the paper form or at the counter. Every order goes into the order book with a number.

## Phone orders

Ask the caller for a name and a phone number before you take any details. Repeat the order back to the caller in full. Take a deposit before you write a party order in the book. Never promise a decorated cake with less than two days of notice. A party order is any order of more than twenty loaves or rolls. For example, forty rolls for a school visit counts as a party order.

## The order book

- Write the pickup date at the top of the page
- Give each order the next number in the book
- Do not use pencil in the order book
- The order book is kept on the shelf under the till

## Changes

Cross out a cancelled order with one straight line and write the reason beside it. Do not tear a page out of the order book. Phone the customer before you change the size of a party order. Case: a party order of thirty loaves was cut to twenty without a call, and the customer arrived to a short count.

## Pickup

Check the order number against the sack before you hand it over. Ask the customer to sign the book for any party order. Unclaimed orders are kept until the next morning and then go to the food bank. The pickup shelf is the narrow shelf beside the street door. Be kind to customers who are in a hurry.

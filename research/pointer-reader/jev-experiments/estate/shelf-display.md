# Shelf display

The front shelves have five levels and each level has a colour tag. Customers read the shelves from the top down, so the best sellers go at eye height.

## Levels

| Level | Tag colour | Goes here |
|---|---|---|
| One | Red | Rye loaves and heavy loaves |
| Two | Blue | Pinnock loaves |
| Three | Green | Rolls and buns |
| Four | Yellow | Cream cakes |

## Rules of the shelf

Put the oldest loaves at the front of each level. Wipe each level with a damp cloth before you refill it. Never place cream cakes on level one, because the rye loaves press into them. Do not cover the price tags with paper sacks. Check the price tag before you put any new kind of loaf on a level. For example, a new walnut loaf needs a fresh tag in the pinnock colour before it goes on level two.

## Gaps

- Fill any gap on levels one to three within ten minutes
- Move a loaf forward when the one behind it sells
- Do not leave a half empty level at the lunch rush
- The lunch rush runs from noon to half past one

## Sales signs

Place the chalk board by the door each morning and write the loaf of the day. Wipe the old chalk board clean before you write on it. The loaf of the day is chosen by the head baker. Case: on a rainy Tuesday the loaf of the day was a large oat loaf and it sold out by ten. Smile at the first customer.

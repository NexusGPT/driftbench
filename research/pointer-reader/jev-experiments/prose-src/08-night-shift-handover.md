# Night-shift handover{{n}}

The evening clerk writes this page before leaving, and the morning clerk reads it before the first call.{{d}} It is short because both of them are tired at the hand-over.{{d}}

**Why it exists.**{{n}} The office has no overlap between shifts.{{d}} Anything not on this page is lost.{{d}}

## Before you leave{{n}}

1. Count the till and write the total at the top of the page.{{r}}
2. List every open refund note and its age in days.{{r}}
3. Write the state of each boat as the harbour master left it.{{r}}
4. Lock the blue shelf and the cash drawer.{{r}}
5. Switch off the quay lights only after the last boat has tied up.{{g}}

Do the till count with a witness whenever the takings are over a hundred pounds.{{r}} A second pair of eyes is the cheapest audit there is.{{d}}

## The board{{n}}

The whiteboard by the door shows the state of each boat in three colours.{{d}} Green means running, amber means delayed, and red means cancelled or paused.{{d}} Wipe it only when the morning clerk has copied it to the page.{{g}}

| Sign{{n}} | Meaning{{n}} | Action{{n}} |
|---|---|---|
| Red marker on the Holm boat{{d}} | Cancelled or paused{{d}} | Tell every Holm booking{{r}} |
| Amber on any boat{{d}} | Delayed{{d}} | Write the delay on the board{{r}} |
| Blue ring round a name{{d}} | Waiting list{{d}} | Ring that person first{{r}} |
| No receipt{{d}} | Takings unclear{{d}} | Stop and ask the other clerk{{r}} |
| Card machine offline{{d}} | Cash only{{d}} | Put the sign in the window{{r}} |

## What the morning clerk finds{{n}}

The office is cold at seven.{{d}} The heater takes twenty minutes, so put it on first and the kettle second.{{r}} The cash drawer is locked and the key is on the hook by the board.{{d}}

Open the log before anything else; a stale log makes everything after it harder.{{g}} Read the refund notes from oldest to newest.{{r}} The oldest notes are usually the angriest.{{d}}

When an entry is unclear, assume the clerk who wrote it knew more than the page shows, and ring them.{{r}} They would rather be rung than have a customer wait.{{d}}

## Handing over the phone{{n}}

The office phone is forwarded to the clerk's mobile after half past five.{{d}} Move the forward to the next clerk before you leave, since a forward that points to a sleeping person is worse than none.{{r}}

If a call comes in while you are handing over, take it; the hand-over can wait.{{r}} Customers on the line are the reason the office exists.{{d}}

## Emergencies at night{{n}}

The harbour emergency number rings through to the harbour master's house.{{d}} It is not for booking questions.{{d}} Give it only for a person in the water, a fire, or a boat aground, never for a late ferry.{{p}}

Phone the number yourself if someone on the quay reports an emergency; do not ask the caller to ring.{{r}} Stay on the line until the harbour master says you can stop.{{r}}

Case: in February a walker slipped on the quay steps and the clerk on duty rang the number herself while a colleague stayed with the walker; the lifeboat was alongside in six minutes.{{e}}

## Cash and cards{{n}}

Takings are counted at close and again at open.{{d}} A difference of more than two pounds is written on the page and shown to the lead.{{r}} Small differences are common on Saturdays, and the lead has never found a pattern.{{d}}

The card machine is a rented device and it connects through the office router.{{d}} It drops offline whenever the router restarts, which the router does at three in the morning.{{d}} Check the machine at seven.{{r}}

Never leave a card receipt face up on the counter overnight.{{p}}

## Notes for next time{{n}}

- Print the fare card in March, not April{{r}}
- Ask the harbour master for the winter pause dates in October{{r}}
- Replace the blue marker, it is nearly dry{{r}}
- Check the glass case on Fridays{{r}}

## Keys{{n}}

The office has three keys: front door, shelf and drawer.{{d}} Keep them on the ring by the board, and sign the key book whenever one leaves the building.{{r}} A key taken home without a signature is a key nobody can find in the morning.{{d}}

The shelf key opens the blue shelf; do not lend it to a walker who says they only want a look.{{p}} Hand over the item instead, and keep the key.{{r}}

The page ends here.{{n}}

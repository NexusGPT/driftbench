# Lost property{{n}}

**What it covers.**{{n}} Bags, coats, phones, bicycle lights and the odd lifejacket left behind by walkers.{{d}} Cash and passports are not covered; they go straight to the office.{{r}}

**The shelf.**{{n}} The office keeps found items on the blue shelf for ninety days.{{d}} After that they go to the island charity shop.{{d}}

## Logging a lost item{{n}}

Ask for four things: what it is, where it was lost, on which sailing, and how to reach the owner.{{r}} Write them into the lost log in that order.{{r}}

Describe the item the way the customer does, not the way you would.{{r}} A "navy bag" and a "dark blue holdall" are the same bag, and the shelf clerk searches by the customer's words.{{d}}

- Item described in the customer's words{{r}}
- Place and sailing{{r}}
- Contact by email or phone, whichever they prefer{{r}}
- Date, written the day the item was lost{{r}}
- Any marks, such as a name tag or a broken zip{{r}}

## Matching{{n}}

A match needs three points to agree: the kind of item, the colour, and the sailing.{{d}} Two points make a maybe, and you pass a maybe to the office rather than tell the customer it is found.{{r}}

Do not describe a found item in more detail than the customer did.{{p}} Someone who claims a bag will volunteer the details, and a stranger will simply agree with yours.{{d}}

Never promise that an item is on the shelf until the clerk has held it in their hands.{{p}}

## Handing back{{n}}

Hand-back happens at the office window, in person, with identification.{{d}} Ask the customer to bring a photo or a receipt if the item is worth more than a night out.{{r}} The clerk signs the log line and the customer signs beside it.{{d}}

For a customer who cannot come in, the office posts small items at the customer's cost.{{d}} Quote the postage from the office, not from memory.{{r}}

## Awkward cases{{n}}

- A child's toy found with no owner → log it, and keep it on the blue shelf for the full ninety days{{r}}
- A phone with a lock screen → do not try to unlock it, hand it to the office{{r}}
- A bag with medicine in it → tell the office at once, as the owner may need it that day{{r}}
- Two people claim the same bag → pass both names to the office and stop{{r}}
- Item found on the quay, not aboard → log the quay and the time, and say it was found on the quay{{r}}

Case: a coat left on the Marrow was matched on colour and sailing alone, handed to the wrong walker, and the real owner came the next morning to an empty hook.{{e}}

## Wording{{n}}

Say "we have logged your item", not "we will find your item".{{r}} Logging is a promise we can keep.{{d}}

The first message to a customer who has lost something is mostly reassurance.{{d}} Tell them what you have written down, and what happens next.{{r}} Customers who feel heard stop sending a second message.{{d}}

You will meet customers who lost something precious, such as a wedding ring.{{d}} For rings, watches and anything with sentimental value, the office wants a phone call, never an email, and the clerk should be told within the hour.{{r}}

## Weekly sweep{{n}}

On Friday the clerk reads the log against the shelf.{{d}} Items with no match after thirty days get a notice in the glass case.{{d}} Items with no owner after ninety days leave the shelf on the first Monday of the month.{{d}}

The sweep cannot be done by Quill; it needs hands and shelves.{{d}} Quill should simply keep the log tidy, so the sweep goes quickly.{{r}}

## Reports{{n}}

Each month the office asks how many items were logged and how many returned.{{d}} The log counts both, and the return rate has stayed near half for two years.{{d}} Do not quote the rate to customers, since it sounds like a poor chance and the real chance depends on the item.{{p}}

Phones and keys come back far more often than scarves and gloves.{{d}}

## Before you close a log line{{n}}

Check the contact detail twice before you close the line.{{g}} A wrong digit in a phone number loses the item for good.{{d}} Do not close a line that has no way to reach the owner.{{p}}

That is all for lost property.{{n}}

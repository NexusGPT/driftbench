Date: 2026-10-15. The owner's ask: "You thoroughly test and properly assume blast radius of those changes across the entire estate (e.g., adapter, CLI, etc.). Make sure that we are ironclad on it."

Scope: every block redesign PR, merged or open, that will merge: 422, 424 (merged), 425, 426, 427, 428, 430 (merged), 431 (merged), 432, 435, 436, 439, 443, 445, 446, 447, 448, 449, 451, 453, 454, 459, 461, 465, 470, and the help-only PRs 455 to 458, 460, 462 to 464, 466, 467, 469, 471, 472. Research PRs 415 to 418, 421, 437, 441, 444 touch only `research/` (0 product files).

Method, per PR: every changed exported symbol, schema key, record field, CLI flag, config key, error or exit code and default, read from the diff against the PR's base; every consumer of each across `packages/*`, `tools/`, `examples/`, `docs/`, `action/`, `python/`, `validators/`, found by name and by key string through re-exports; for each consumer, the test that exercises it with the new behaviour, or a new test. Every new test went red on a planted mutation of its consumer, and the consumer file was restored byte for byte. No test makes a paid or network call.

## Interactions and carry-forward rules

- **422 + 432:** PR 432's `adapters/src/shared/history.ts:50` sets `UNRECORDED_OUTPUT = null`, so merging 432 as it stands undoes 422; its `history-wires.ts` helper still expects `null` for a call with no result. The pr-422 runner test goes red on that merge. Rebase 432 onto 422 and carry the note into the compiler.
- **428 + 449, record version:** 449 adds `by`, `resolved`, `diagnoses`, `skippedForBudget` without a bump; only 428 moves `SCHEMA_VERSION` to 3. 449 must land with or after 428, or an older driftbench reports new records as corrupt.
- **428 + 465:** `rescoreRun` (465) has no guard for not_run or blocked blocks; 428 adds one to `rejudgeRun` only. After both merge, a not_run block would be re-scored.
- **426 + 428, rate denominators:** `report/src/headline.ts:75, 81, 94-98` (the suite headline, pooled rate, n_eff, design effect, detectable drop) divide by every run; `headline.ts:77` counts blocked and not_run twice in "did not complete"; `html.ts:232` prints n_eff beside rates over every run. 428 holds neither 425 nor 426, so its denominator fixes do not reach these lines. Every rate gets the 1 pass, 1 not_run, 1 blocked test expecting 100% of scored when both are in one tree.
- **B4 rates (454):** the two scores leave failed and stopped runs out of both denominators and fold `unassigned` into neither; extend the test with not_run and blocked once 428 is under them.
- **436:** sits on 428's old head 4434c3c28 without 431; rebase before its stacked tests merge.
- **Help PRs:** 455, 457, 458, 460, 462 to 464, 466, 467, 469 touch only `packages/cli/help`. 456 sits on a pre-rebase 422 head. 471 and 472 carry stale, non-equivalent copies of the B2 and B3 product commits: merged as they stand they bring old product code back.
- **Phase 6 links (from the PR 423 audit, carry-forward):** a spec storing `metadata.provenance` changed hash under 423 (the store refused it) and free-form `metadata.links` stopped validating. Rule for the redone design: links and provenance get their own top-level keys (`links:` on the spec and the block), `metadata` stays free and stays in the hash, and a store row written before must read back at its stored hash. The two expected-fail cases stay on branch `test/block-blast-radius-423` (no PR).
- **Frozen snapshots:** `examples/acme/gated` and `examples/acme/golden` are frozen copies, never regenerated by a feature PR. Their 58 sealed holdout hashes are the point of a sealed set; `examples/acme/test/blast-seal-hashes.test.ts` fails if any sealed spec gains a key or moves its hash.
- **Equivalent mutant noted, not removed:** `packages/cli/src/commands/campaign.ts:888` passes `recordRealizedText` into the block-rerun planner's options, which `planBlockRerun` never reads. The reruns run through `runCampaign`, which the radius test covers.
- **Existing tests slow under load, not failing:** `campaign-provenance.test.ts` (5 s default), `api-surface.test.mjs` and `family.test.ts` timed out on the loaded audit machine and pass with longer timeouts and on CI.

# Fictional Zed sampling fixture

This fixture measures numeric sampling only. It does not call a model or score faults.
Stages, in order: greet, help, done. The balance flow follows all three stages.
Channels: chat and email. Personas: novice, regular and expert.
Temperature ranges from 0 to 1. Delay ranges from 0 to 100 milliseconds.
Iterations range from 1 to 5 integer attempts.
